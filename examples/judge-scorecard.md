### JUDGE SCORECARD — scoring a nine-step run

Use this when several teams run the chain on the same brief, or when one piece of work needs a number. Score each step 0 to 10. Write the "why" next to every score; the number without the reason teaches nobody. Average the rows for a judge total, average the judges for the final score.

The questions under each step are the rubric. They are the same tests the method applies to itself, compressed to what a judge can hold in the room.

| # | Step | What to evaluate | Judging guidance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Zeitgeist | Does the team set up a recognisable cultural context the audience already understands? | Specific and culturally alive, not generic? Brings the brand into the world's conversation, not the world to the brand? Could you picture this moment in real life? |
| 2 | Tension | Is there a clear, sharp conflict or contradiction inside the Zeitgeist? | Externally focused (a problem of the world, not just the brand)? Evocative: does the room react? Stateable in one sentence? Does solving it actually matter? |
| 3 | Insight | Does the insight reframe the problem with an "aha"? | Recognition over education ("yes, that's true", not "oh, I didn't know"). Explains the system or rule behind the behaviour, not just the behaviour (Beard Test). Invisible before, obvious after. |
| 4 | Role | Is the brand's specific superpower clear and earned? | Higher-order benefit, not a feature? Could only this brand credibly own this Role? Connects directly to the Insight? |
| 5 | Call to Action | Is the desired audience action clear, simple and single-minded? | Explainable in one sentence? Goes beyond brand and customer? Easy to follow and act on? |
| 6 | Strategy (GWTB) | Does the GWTB hold together as one flowing sentence? | GET is specific, not a demographic? WHO or FROM feels real, not corporate? TO is observable behaviour, not a KPI? BY sparks a visual idea? Reads as natural human speech? |
| 7 | Creative | Does the creative express the strategy in a way that makes you feel something? | Post-It test: one sentence? Camouflage test: could only be this brand? Generates other ideas (a platform, not a tagline)? Distinctive, not merely memorable? |
| 8 | Big Idea | Does one execution carry the whole campaign? | Stand-alone test: can someone understand the campaign from this one execution alone? Visualisable in one image? Single-minded? |
| 9 | Small Ideas | Do the tactics belong to the same idea and surround the moment? | Each tactic feels like the same campaign? Covers PESO or the customer journey? Integrated, not disconnected? |
| 10 | Overall | Would you run it? | Logic and poetry both present? Would you actually run this campaign? Could competitors copy it (camouflage test)? Does it feel like the team understood the brief, or just answered it? |

**Scoring notes**

- A 10 means the step could be lifted into a case file as is. A 5 means the step exists but a judge has to do the work to see it. A 0 means the step is missing or is a different step wearing the wrong label.
- Score the chain, not the deck. A beautiful slide with a weak Insight scores on the Insight.
- When two judges differ by more than three points on a step, they talk before the average is taken. The disagreement is usually a Logic Stack problem, not a taste problem.
