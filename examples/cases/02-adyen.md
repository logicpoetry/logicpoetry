#### Case 2: Adyen (Fintech / B2B Payments)

**Zeitgeist**: Tech success stories are always framed as "better/faster/cheaper" — companies win when the software outperforms the competition on measurable metrics.

**Tension**: The people who actually use the technology — the operators, the CRM specialists, the frontline staff — rarely appear in the success story. The company wins; the individual disappears.

**Insight**: True ambition is when no person is left behind in the digital transformation. The real Adyen story isn't the platform — it's the people the platform enables to become more than what they were.

**Subject Flip**: Narrative shifts from corporate KPIs → individual employee growth

**Creative Platform**: #ReimagineAmbition

**Campaign Narrative Example — "Shu Shan"**:
Profile story of a CRM specialist at Love Bonito whose role evolved as the company scaled on Adyen's infrastructure. She didn't just use a tool — her job description changed. Her ambitions grew. Adyen's platform didn't just process payments; it redefined what her career could look like.

**Big Idea**: Reframe Adyen's product roadshow as a "job mentorship pop-up" at prospect office lobbies. Employees fill out reimagined role forms + headshots, compiled into a booklet formatted as "job applications for roles that don't exist yet" — delivered to the CEO as a trojan horse for C-suite awareness without requiring direct customer approval.

**Small Ideas**:
- #ReimagineAmbition Council — executive roundtable → whitepaper + executive bylines + video series
- Always-on social with employee-featured stories
- Video series: "The Role That Didn't Exist Before"

**Campaign Roadmap**: Awareness → Education → Consideration → Conversion

**Why It Works** (6 criteria):
1. Journalist-friendly human angle — it's a people story, not a payments story
2. Future of Work hook — organic to a major ongoing media narrative
3. National agenda alignment — SG workforce upskilling is a government priority; Adyen can ride that tailwind
4. ESG/DEI angle — inclusive digital transformation is a credible corporate responsibility story
5. Sidesteps customer approval bottleneck — employees self-submit, bypassing standard approvals
6. Complementary to existing equity — "Reimagine Ambition" extends rather than contradicts "Engineered for Ambition"
