#### Case 7: Grab Ads (B2B / AdTech)

**Zeitgeist**: Media planning is on autopilot. Budgets default to Google and Meta. Agency planning decks look the same every year. The playbook is set.

**Tension**: Grab is positioned as a super-app that does everything — rides, food, delivery, payments, insurance. From an AdTech perspective, "everything" isn't a differentiator. It's a category blur.

**Insight**: Grab's superpower isn't breadth — it's *moment*. Grab is present at the exact moments when people are deciding, moving, and acting. GrabAds has the unique ability to reach someone when they are *already* in motion toward a decision. It doesn't interrupt. It accompanies. That's the power to influence everyone's next step.

**Role**: GrabAds as the platform for capturing the intent moment — not the browsing moment, not the scrolling moment, but the *doing* moment.

**Creative Platform**: "Next Step"

**Multi-audience platform flex** — same platform, three different messages:
- **To Advertisers**: "Be there when your customers are ready to take that next step."
- **To AdTech community**: "GrabAds is the next step in the ad space."
- **To Media Agencies**: "GrabAds is the next step in your media strategy."

**GWTB**:
```
WHO form:
GET media planners at agencies and brand marketing teams
WHO default to the same Google/Meta playbook because "it's safe"
TO add GrabAds to the consideration set as the intent-moment platform
BY showing that they're missing the one moment that matters most — when the customer is already moving

FROM form:
GET media planners and brand marketers
FROM treating Grab as a lifestyle app, not an AdTech platform
TO recognizing GrabAds as a uniquely powerful tool for capturing intent at the decision moment
BY making the next step — literally — the campaign
```

**Big Idea**: Guerrilla campaign targeting media planners. Offline: Grab car wraps circling agency office buildings at peak planning hours (morning standup, evening wrap) with Singlish copy — "OT-ing again because of the media plan ah? Next step: GrabAds." Online: time-triggered in-app ads at 7pm and 10pm targeting agency professionals finishing late — same message, different execution.

**Small Ideas** (organized by audience segment):
- *For agency planners*: Partnership with @JustMediaThingss (agency meme account) — sponsored content that speaks the planner's language; trade press pitch on hyper-local data
- *For brands*: "Next Step Newsroom" — always-on trends TL powered by Grab's first-party data; "Next Step Barometer" — proprietary brand data product
- *For SMBs*: "Superlocal" — simplified GrabAds entry point for small businesses
- *For all*: "Media Superclass" — educational event + "Super Sales Kit" (sales enablement materials)

**Why It Works** (3 criteria):
1. Clear differentiator — intent-moment positioning is something Google/Meta/everyone else cannot own as credibly
2. Plays to genuine strengths — Grab's hyper-local, real-behavior data is genuinely differentiated
3. CTA "Next Step" works across all channels and all audiences without needing adaptation

**Creative Moves in play**: Multi-audience platform flex; product-as-proof (GrabAds selling GrabAds via GrabAds); B2B guerrilla with cultural humor
