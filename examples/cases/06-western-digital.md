#### Case 6: Western Digital (B2B Tech / Storage)

**Zeitgeist**: Storage is a commodity conversation. Terabytes, price-per-GB, read/write speeds. Buyers compare specs. Sellers compete on price. The category races to the bottom.

**Tension**: The actual conversation enterprises and creators are having — owned vs. cloud, control vs. cost, compliance vs. convenience — is fundamentally more complex and strategic than hardware specs. But the industry keeps selling on specs.

**Insight**: "Cloud-first does not mean cloud-only." Most organizations discover — often painfully — that pure cloud has a ceiling: cost at scale, latency, control, data sovereignty. That ceiling has a name now: the **Cloud Ceiling**.

**Role**: WD is not a hardware vendor. WD is the strategic CDO/CTO who helps organizations architect the right storage mix — and break through the Cloud Ceiling without blowing up the budget or the architecture.

**Creative Platform**: "Your Data, Your Story"

**GWTB (FROM)**:
```
GET data-driven creators, enterprises, and CTOs
FROM viewing WD as just another hardware provider in a commoditized spec race
TO recognizing WD as a strategic ally in designing storage architectures that work
BY coining and owning the "Cloud Ceiling" conversation — and positioning WD as the way through it
```

**Big Idea**: "Breaking Through the Cloud Ceiling" — a flagship research report quantifying the hidden costs organizations hit when they go pure-cloud. Launched with a media and analyst tour; WD becomes the source of record for hybrid storage economics.

**Small Ideas**:
- Creative professional campaign: "Your Data, Your Story" — stories of creators and enterprises who reclaimed control of their work when they stopped outsourcing their storage decisions
- WD as conference keynote voice on hybrid storage architecture
- Always-on content series featuring real CTOs navigating the cloud ceiling moment

**Creative Moves in play**: Category reframing; coined concept as campaign anchor (Cloud Ceiling)
