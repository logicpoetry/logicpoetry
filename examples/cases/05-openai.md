#### Case 5: OpenAI (Tech / Corporate Narrative)

**Zeitgeist**: AI is the dominant conversation of the decade. Every company is claiming intelligence, capability, openness, and responsibility — often simultaneously, often without definition.

**Tension**: OpenAI's own name creates an unavoidable question the brand cannot escape: what does "open" actually mean? It can't mean fully open-source. It can't mean free. But it can't mean nothing either — the name is too prominent to leave undefined.

**Insight**: "Open" doesn't have to mean absolute. It can mean directional. Open as a journey — toward greater transparency, access, and shared benefit — is a more honest and more defensible claim than any fixed definition.

**Role**: OpenAI as the company that is *actively becoming* more open — not one that simply declared itself so.

**Creative Platform**: "Open is a Journey"

**GWTB**:
```
WHO form:
GET the public, developers, and policymakers
WHO are skeptical about what "open" means from a company that isn't fully open-source
TO see OpenAI as the most honest voice about the complexity of openness in AI
BY making the journey — not the destination — the proof of the commitment

FROM form:
GET skeptics and curious adopters
FROM seeing "OpenAI" as a contradiction or a marketing claim
TO believing openness is something OpenAI is genuinely working toward
BY showing every product decision as a step on a journey, not a finished state
```

**Big Idea**: GPT Live! Experience — interactive installations (billboards, activations) powered by localized customGPTs. The technology demonstrates openness by being available, responsive, and real — in the public space.

**Manifesto** (Register A — Philosophical/Corporate):
What does "open" mean? It means the door is not locked. It means the question is welcome. It means the work is never finished. Open is not a product feature. It is not a terms and conditions page. Open is a direction. A commitment to move toward — not arrive at. Every model we release is a step. Every API we make available is a door we chose not to lock. Every answer we give is a chance to be more honest than we were before. OpenAI. Open is a journey.

**Campaign Roadmap** (3 phases, ~12 months):
1. Build Foundation — establish the "Open is a Journey" narrative; seed with media and developer community
2. Build Relevance — product launches and feature releases framed as journey milestones
3. Build Preference — GPT Live! Experience activations; case studies from the journey

**Creative Moves in play**: Product-as-proof (used ChatGPT to develop the strategy); corporate name liability → brand story
