### CLUSTER E: PROOF & EVIDENCE

Techniques using data, demonstration, or real evidence as creative material.

**E1. The Data Portrait (Behavioral Mirror)**
Transform aggregate behavioral data into creative that reflects the audience back at themselves.
- Spotify "Thanks 2016" — "Dear person who played 'Sorry' 42 times on Valentine's Day…"

**E2. Mass Intimacy (Personalized-Feeling at Scale)**
A fixed phrase with a variable slot makes mass media feel personal.
- Share a Coke — names on bottles (agency: Ogilvy Sydney)
- Spotify Wrapped — "You're in the top 0.01%"

**E3. Documentary-Style Proof**
Convert a claim into a visible experiment.
- "Real Beauty Sketches" — Dove (agency: Ogilvy Brazil)

**E4. Data-as-Proof**
Real behavioral data as evidence of impact.
- "The Truth Is Worth It" — NYT (agency: Droga5)
- "Parisian Love" — Google

**E5. Proof Through Repulsion**
Show the ugly truth. The disgust IS the evidence.
- Moldy Whopper — Burger King
