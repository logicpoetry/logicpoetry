### CLUSTER D: CULTURE & REFERENCE

Techniques that borrow from cultural artifacts, vernacular, or shared knowledge.





**D3. Real-Time Moment Hijack**
Speed + relevance. The line belongs to the event.
- "You can still dunk in the dark" — Oreo (Super Bowl blackout; agency: 360i)

**D4. Cultural Callback / News-Mimicry**
Evoke cultural touchstones or link to current news.
- Amul's weekly billboards — India (decades of topical commentary)

**D5. The Ritual Creation**
Invent a new behavior associated with the brand.
- "Twist, Lick, Dunk" — Oreo
- Spotify Wrapped — annual sharing ritual

**D6. Cultural Judo**
Ride an existing cultural force and steer it.
- "#LikeAGirl" — Always (redirected the phrase's energy)
- "Value the Australian Way" — Coles (reframed "value" itself)

**D7. The Language Rescue (Untranslatable Word)**
Adopt an untranslatable word from another language as brand territory.
- "Fahrvergnügen" — Volkswagen (1990)
