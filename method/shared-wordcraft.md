# Shared wordcraft

At Steps 7–9, use wordcraft when expression, image contribution or sequence needs work. Fix facts, audience, surface and voice. Generate a plain baseline plus relevant word-operator alternatives; judge meaning before sound. Develop words and scene together. Preserve checkpoints, approved material and deck register. Truth and comprehension are gates. Skip formatting-only work. The full recipe follows in the reader edition; a runtime-only caller can use this compact loop without fetching another file.

## Entry contract

**Identity:** `LP-APPLE-R11` names this combined recipe; `LP-APPLE-WG01` names its operator crosswalk. They extend an Apple communication research companion, while the recipe's maintained portable source is this module. The complete research archive remains separate. This is a practical synthesis, not Apple's internal process, a Google product or a measured guarantee of better copy.

**When:** a naming, headline, verbal-identity or expressive-copy task; a word/image relationship needing development; a reveal or sequence needing a clearer handoff. In a campaign, apply within the current Step 7, 8 or 9 assignment and its candidate budget. Direct writing can use it without starting a campaign. In Spreads, evaluate it at the copy/image brief, narrative sequencing and combined-output review. The user need not say “Apple.”

**When to skip or narrow:** exact transcription, approved wording, formatting-only fixes or exact asset replacement. UI, specifications, eligibility, prices and instructions normally need literal clarity; use the review gates without forcing wordplay. Research intake alone does not start generation. Rough storyboards may explore concepts; finishing must wait for the production workflow's approval and asset gates. Do not use this recipe to unlock settled copy.

**Voice:** select the destination brand and register first. Apple-inspired mechanics can serve a warm, technical, playful or sober voice. Deck titles, bands and body remain conversational under Soundcheck; Apple-style lines belong in pull-out quotes only when explicitly requested. This recipe grants no publication permission and bypasses neither human checkpoint.

## LP-APPLE-R11 — Generate, develop, compare

1. **Fix the proposition.** Record audience, desired understanding/action, surface, language/locale, exact supported claim, qualifications, available assets and approved wording. Write one plain baseline. If the brief lacks a product fact, leave a gap; invention is permitted in labelled concepts, not evidence.
2. **Choose useful searches.** Name what is missing: a second meaning, a concrete scene, memorable sound, a useful analogy or a better reveal. Choose a few relevant operators from `LP-APPLE-WG01`. Keep one plain route and materially different starting points. No requirement to run all fifteen or to add candidates beyond the current stage's budget.
3. **Branch before combining.** For each attempt record seed → qualified operator → intermediate material → proposed expression. Explore unrelated starts before refining a favourite. For similar-sounding words, say the seed aloud, split/resegment or vary sounds, then recover a meaningful phrase. A spelling coincidence alone is insufficient. Discard stretches that require an explanation to sound alike in the intended accent.
4. **Develop a relationship.** Connect promising material to a benefit, human situation or organising idea. Use a relevant craft mechanism below. Combine operators only when the intermediate output gives the next one something useful to transform. Keep the literal proposition visible so a rhyme cannot change the promise. A raw phrase is a seed; a campaign platform must still generate different executions.
5. **Assign each medium a job.** Write “Words say X; image adds Y; together they mean Z.” Choose whether the image completes meaning, shows evidence, locates a situation or reveals a consequence. Plan crop, scale, line breaks and reading order. For motion, give each beat an action, verbal contribution and reveal. Specify what music, effects, silence and voice add. Essential meaning needs an accessible alternative; do not claim footage or audio was reviewed from its description alone.
6. **Edit for the chosen surface.** Remove surplus setup, choose concrete verbs and read aloud. Preserve necessary conditions, units and comparisons. A headline can defer explanation to adjacent body; a control must predict its action. A teaser can withhold a reveal; purchasing and safety information cannot depend on that game. Check line breaks in the actual composition, not only in a text editor.
7. **Compare with the baseline.** Apply the hard gates and tunable preferences below. Keep the plain version when the device adds little. Show finalists with their scene, mechanism, gain/loss and unresolved checks. Human preference selects direction; audience comprehension and response still require testing. Do not report an “Apple fidelity” percentage from self-scoring.

## Craft mechanisms, available without the research archive

| Companion reference | Procedure | Failure check |
|---|---|---|
| LP-APPLE-T01 / LP-APPLE-T25 | Keep one central proposition; remove words that do not change meaning, guidance or rhythm | Compression removes a qualification or makes the reader guess |
| LP-APPLE-T02 | Map product facts, human benefits, cultural language and sensory associations; search overlaps | Category clichés replace a specific claim |
| LP-APPLE-T03 / LP-APPLE-T04 | Take a relevant familiar phrase literally, or change one sound/word to create a second relevant reading | Recognition is too slow or the new reading says nothing useful |
| LP-APPLE-T05 / LP-APPLE-T06 | Repeat a word with a changed sense, or use parallel syntax to clarify a meaningful contrast | Decorative antithesis or repetition disguises an empty proposition |
| LP-APPLE-T09 / LP-APPLE-T10 | Tune stress, consonants, vowels and word formation after meaning works | Sound dominates intelligibility or brand fit |
| LP-APPLE-T13 / LP-APPLE-T18 / LP-APPLE-T32 / LP-APPLE-T33 | Make the benefit concrete through a sensation, human scene, context or visual analogy | A symbolic scene is presented as measured product evidence |
| LP-APPLE-T16 / LP-APPLE-T20 / LP-APPLE-T30 | Explain mechanism → consequence and pair the scoped claim with appropriate proof or a demonstration | A render, analogy or controlled demo implies unproved real-world performance |

## Controls and decision gates

**Hard gates:** factual entailment; understandable intended reading; required qualifications; usable action; approved-content and voice constraints; accessibility. A failure is a reason to repair or reject, regardless of a cleverness score.

**Preferences:** set low/medium/high for semantic distance, wordplay, sonic pattern, compression, warmth, technical explicitness, image dependence and reveal delay. Change one or two while keeping the proposition fixed. There is no universal best setting. If numeric weights help compare survivors, record them as the user's preferences, not measured probabilities; never allow a weighted average to hide a failed gate.

Example starting profiles: technical explanation = low wordplay, high explicitness, low reveal delay; expressive hero = moderate wordplay, high compression, paired visual; spoken script = moderate compression with deliberate rhythm and breath. These are adjustable hypotheses. Translation requires new word searches and local listening checks, not literal transfer of a pun.

## Worked example: a sound route that loses to meaning

**Fictional brief:** a desk lamp has adjustable brightness. No battery, energy-saving or automatic-adaptation claims are supplied. Intended job: show that a reader can set the light for the task. Warm, plain voice. Still hero with adjacent body.

| Attempt | Lineage and candidate | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Baseline | Literal proposition → “Set the light for the task.” | Clear control promise; keep as benchmark |
| Phrase route | LP-CORE-W10: light → “light reading” → LP-APPLE-T05: two relevant senses → “Light for light reading. And the heavy kind.” | Possible playful hero; depends on immediate context and may be longer than useful |
| Sound route | LP-CORE-W02: bright → “write” near-sound material → “Write light.” | Reject: weak sound match, unclear meaning and no meaningful benefit beyond the baseline |
| Scene route | LP-CORE-W09 → LP-APPLE-T18: reader adjusts the lamp for a task | Keep for visual development without requiring an expressive headline |

For the phrase route, the image shows the same person choosing brightness while moving between reading tasks; body says “Adjust the brightness to suit what you're doing.” Keep ambient light and exposure honest if comparing output. The images add context and visible control; they do not prove reduced eye strain. A motion version can show adjustment before revealing the line; a silent version must still convey control. These are proposed executions, not tested audience findings or Apple examples.

## Deliver and learn

Return the useful copy/composition first, then a compact record: fixed proposition; surface and voice; selected controls; source → operator → craft mechanism → candidate; what each medium adds; why the winner beats or loses to the baseline; remaining evidence and production checks. Retain useful rejected paths with the reason, so future passes do not repeat them unknowingly.

To test whether this combination helps, compare the baseline and combined-method outputs on held-out briefs across headlines, body, UI and multimodal work. Randomise presentation, ask target readers to paraphrase the claim and predict the action, then assess voice fit and preference. Record truth errors, misunderstanding and revision effort alongside preference. No improvement claim is warranted from this recipe's existence or one self-reviewed example.
