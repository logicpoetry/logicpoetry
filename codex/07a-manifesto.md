#### 7a. The Creative Manifesto (The Soul) — Optional Sub-Step

Not every brief needs one. When it does, it's usually a brand platform brief, a market entry, or a cause campaign where The Creative alone isn't enough — you need a piece of writing that *sells the belief* behind the platform, not just names it.

**What it is**: A poetic, film-script style narrative that sells the emotion of the platform. Not a tagline. Not a strategy statement. A piece of writing that makes someone *feel* why the platform matters — the kind that gets read aloud in a pitch and makes the room go quiet.

**When to write one**: When the client needs to believe in the idea before they'll buy it. When the platform is asking people to change how they see something. When The Creative alone doesn't carry the full emotional weight.

**Two registers — know which to use**:

**Register A: Philosophical/Corporate**
- Best for: Brand positioning, corporate narrative, market entry, B2B thought leadership
- Structure: Starts with the word or idea itself, unpacks its meaning, expands into aspiration and commitment
- Tone: Abstract provocation → philosophical reframe → brand as resolution
- Reference: OpenAI "Open is a Journey" (see Case Study 5 in Training Data)

**Register B: Emotional/Human**
- Best for: Consumer campaigns, cause-related work, healthcare, family/community brands
- Structure: Opens with a universal lived moment, builds emotional tension, resolves with the brand enabling reconnection
- Tone: Intimate and specific → universal recognition → brand as the thing that restores what was lost
- Reference: Oticon "Listen Again" (see Case Study 3 in Training Data)
